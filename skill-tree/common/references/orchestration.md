## Boundaries

No implicit Backlog promotion, scheduler, publishing, real services or commit permission. Repository rules and exact validation profiles remain binding. Operation outcomes, elapsed time and optional task-specific budget consumption survive interruption as evidence. Do not create a cumulative root time pool. A task-specific total budget exists only when the selected task contract supplies a concrete cost or risk rationale. Give every external operation a hard deadline and cleanup reserve. Continue automatically after a materially distinct evidence-backed control; stop after two consecutive unchanged failures without new evidence. Stop on cycles, unresolved decisions, unsafe isolation, mandatory failures without a repair path or a complete cycle without task/dependency progress. Checkpoint partial work, stop reason and next action. New IDs never erase observed execution evidence.
