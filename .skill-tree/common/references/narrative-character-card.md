# Causal character identity card

A useful identity card predicts plausible choices without making personality deterministic. Pin the source material and separate identity from temporary knowledge, role and appearance.

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Identity and position | Stable ID, recognizable behavior, role, resources and limits |
| Goals and values | Immediate want, durable commitment, obligations and a boundary the character will defend |
| History and relationships | Events that explain behavior, dependencies on other people and current stakes |
| Knowledge and belief | Established knowledge, available evidence, suspicion and any mistaken belief |
| Tactics and voice | Observable methods under pressure, ways of speaking and evidence for an exception |
| Arc | Possible change, stable commitment, refusal to change or decline |

Convert a label such as protective into an action, a reason and an exception. A character can bargain, measure, delay, shield or confront. Name the circumstances in which another tactic becomes credible. Distinguish a motivational weakness from a physical restriction and a missing source from an intentional false belief.
