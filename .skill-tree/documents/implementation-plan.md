## Internal phases and decision governance

Both modes use research, plan, plan review, implementation, required checks, review, finding resolution, fixes and re-review. The phases initially remain inside existing operations. A clean review completes without inventing a finding or a fix. Standalone planning/report commands and new release/retrospective operations remain outside the initial operation set.

Auto is the approved default. The executor supplies the question, options, recommendation and researched facts. A separate resolver chooses an option with a recorded rationale. Significant decisions receive an independent critic; substantive disagreement receives an arbiter. Required actors use distinct recorded contexts. A missing required resolver/critic/arbiter capability records unavailable/prerequisite evidence, preserves the unresolved decision and any task hold, and produces one concrete owner request or waits for an explicit Manual switch. The executor cannot supply a fabricated independent role. Manual sends the same question and recommendation to the owner in chat and awaits an actual answer at the decision checkpoint. Routine authorized actions continue between checkpoints.

[W1 policy](plugin-workflow-decisions.md#approved-decision-w1-internal-phases-and-decision-modes) bounds delegated product/technical choices and final specification acceptance by agreed goals, scope and constraints. Decision records retain the real actor, authority, rationale and review evidence. Out-of-authority requests and repeated no-progress disputes/failures escalate one concrete owner question. Agent agreement cannot replace required checks or applicable independent approval. Existing owner authorizations remain valid.

Project defaults support explicit per-run overrides. Effective configuration freezes selected mode, delegation, bindings, revisions and required capabilities. An explicit mode switch records a guarded transition and preserves accumulated task/session/run state. Changing project defaults never silently changes a parked run. A mode switch supplies no missing answer, successful check or expanded authority. Switching reconciles or explicitly cancels live actors under the original attempt/configuration before using the new mode. Late replies remain historical or stale evidence and cannot answer a new-mode question. Historical answers and receipts remain available, while readiness requires current-mode, current-authority acceptance; delegated Auto acceptance alone cannot satisfy Manual owner confirmation.
