# Balance study for one design decision

Start from a selected rule, a clip or a configuration extract. Identify the experience or system outcome the owner wants to change, the decision a comparison can inform and the controlled scenario. Use the [shared record contract](game-design-records.md) for exact rule, placement, study and check revisions. A sparse input can yield a useful provisional study or measurement plan.

## Record the comparison

| Field | Required decision context |
| --- | --- |
| Question and owner | Name the intended outcome, the parameter or behavior under review and the rule owner authorized to adopt a change. |
| Conditions | Pin the relevant rule, placements, mode, population or policy, starting state and build or operating context. State controlled inputs and remaining assumptions. |
| Measure | Define the observation, unit, clock and direction of improvement. For rates or proportions, specify the exposure, denominator, eligible attempts and counting rule. |
| Baseline and candidate | Record the authoritative baseline or the exact observed baseline with provenance and status. Name one candidate change, expected consequence and competing explanation. |
| Method | State the calculation, event sequence or observation procedure. Record enough inputs, conditions, rounding and evidence references for an independent reconstruction. |
| Uncertainty and guardrail | Name the assumption or variation that could change the conclusion. Choose an adverse outcome to watch and the result or condition that stops the comparison. |
| Outcome and follow-up | Report the comparison or evidence gap, exact check attempts, affected placements, recommendation and owner decision status. Link earlier check attempts with the original scope. |

Choose a method after identifying the question, clock and evidence. A known rate and capacity can support a dimensional calculation under static conditions. A discrete chance rule can support enumeration of eligible outcomes. Continuous or unattended behavior can require a time trace with caps, pauses and persistence. A player-facing question about noticing a cue or adopting a choice requires observation under comparable exposure. Select a threshold and sample based on the decision and the consequences of an error.

## Preserve evidence and authority

- A paper comparison states the expected outcome under explicit assumptions and uses units consistent with the pinned inputs.
- Configuration inspection identifies the selected setting and the effective source, overrides and owner for the named environment.
- Gameplay evidence records observed behavior, starting conditions and eligible attempts on the selected build or Editor session.
- Human response records what participants noticed, attempted or understood under the recorded conditions.

Compare evidence within the applicable conditions. Changing the build, rules, cohort, exposure or prior state can explain a difference. Preserve an unresolved competing cause or uncertainty when the current checks cannot discriminate. Retain the original attempts and observe affected placements again after an approved change.

When a baseline or design target is missing, retain the known facts and assumptions. Specify who can supply the approved intent and the smallest observation that can establish an effective baseline, including settings, start state, unit and eligible exposure. Report a measurement plan while the decision remains open.

When the effective setting disagrees with the accepted specification, pin both revisions and the observed override source. Ask the configuration owner to verify the effective assignment and capture a comparable control. Route a proposed design change to the rule owner. Numerical comparisons remain proposals until the rule owner accepts an exact revision. A rejection records the candidate and reason and preserves the accepted value and prior evidence.

## Deliver the reusable study

Return a scoped study with the decision, inputs, result or measurement plan, risks and named next owner. Include the parameter source, affected placement references and the next discriminating check. Direct Balance work can finish with a study document or open measurement plan within the consuming project.

Use the [Balance methods by decision](game-design-balance-methods.md) reference to select a check for the relevant clock, population and risk.

Use the [focused playtest protocol](game-design-playtest.md) when a tuning comparison needs direct observations of player choice or adoption.
