# Human Narrative Playtest

Before play record consent, exact build, work and initial state, targeted route, facilitator role, requested task and a stopping condition. The facilitator gives only information a real player would have at the starting point and records any later intervention.

Observe the first decision without explaining the intended interpretation. Record offered choices, player action, stated intent, expected outcome, noticed cues and hesitation with the relevant beat. After play ask what the player expected, what appeared to cause the event, and which cue supported the inference. Use open questions and record the player's own words.

Separate action logs, participant quotes, facilitator intervention and design hypotheses. Compare main, alternate and skip observations only within the covered sample. Distinguish a participant choosing a path from the facilitator directing the path. The protocol establishes no audience-wide conclusion.
