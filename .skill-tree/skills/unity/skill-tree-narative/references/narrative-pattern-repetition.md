# Review Dramatic Recurrence Across Episodes

Declare exact works, scenes, revisions and routes before comparing recurring patterns. A scene outside the declared sample contributes no coverage. Start with [actionable dramaturgical review](narrative-dramaturgical-review.md) for local observations.

For each relevant beat record mechanism, intended function, disclosure timing, consequence and intended motif. Compare the underlying mechanism rather than names, genre decoration or location. Keep recurrence that changes meaning or consequence distinct from repeated surprise with equivalent timing and effect.

State observed counts with the sample denominator. Cite each relevant scene. Offer alternatives such as a sincere mistake, competing obligation, care, shame or a conflict of values. Consider costs, canon and route effects before recommending a change. Record accepted or rejected options and the reason in the author decision context when actual choices exist.
