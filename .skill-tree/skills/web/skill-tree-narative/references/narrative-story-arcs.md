# Work-Level Story Arcs

A story arc connects a thematic question and a sequence of meaningful changes across a work. Start with the work profile, intended experience, continuity and permitted predecessor outcomes. Keep unresolved owner choices visible. Use a composition model only when the model helps diagnose the selected arc.

## Build the arc map

Identify each milestone by a stable event or authoring ID. State the causal basis, actor or player action and result. Record how each scene or quest changes the conditions for the next relevant milestone. A chronology orders events in the world. A disclosure map identifies when an audience learns about events. A route map records where the player can act or proceed. Keep all three views distinct.

Classify material by function. Fixed anchors occur across admitted routes. Variable approaches change evidence, resources or relations while retaining a destination. Optional enrichments add interpretation or tone. A required fact or transition carried only by optional material needs another source or explicit mandatory status.

Map interleaved threads by permissible order and blocking dependencies. At convergence record any carried trust, losses, knowledge or restrictions. Compare route-level consequences rather than counting content nodes. A stable character can retain a value while costs rise.

## Synthetic arc map

The work asks whether a public warning should protect privacy or maximize immediate reach. A warden remains committed to rescue across the arc.

| Milestone | Basis | Material and change | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `arc-warning` | The tide register crosses the public threshold. | The arrival scene presents the evacuation objective. | Fixed event. |
| `arc-authorization` | The player obtains a dock permit from a receipt or supervised inspection. | The inspection quest grants access and records different trust. | Variable approach. |
| `arc-disclosure` | A public bulletin proves the deadline. | An optional witness adds context about why the deadline was concealed. | Bulletin required. Witness optional. |
| `arc-evacuation` | The permit and known deadline make a crossing plan available. | The player chooses public or limited warning. The chosen publicity and retained trust shape the reunion. | Fixed destination with varied outcomes. |

A maintenance inquiry can interleave with the permit quest before evacuation. The bulletin and permit remain required. If the optional witness becomes the sole source of the deadline, either relocate a usable basis to the main route or require the witness explicitly. Preserve the desired witness ambiguity where the bulletin supplies the actionable fact.
