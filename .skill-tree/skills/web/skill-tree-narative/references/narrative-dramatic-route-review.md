# Setup and Payoff Route Review

Start with the exact candidate, pin and [dramatic links](narrative-dramatic-links.md). Identify the changed setup, reveal, reframe or payoff and state whether the intent is optional enrichment or required comprehension. Keep the playable gate separate.

## Review cases

| Route case | Check |
| --- | --- |
| Seen | Confirm when the cue appears, which holder can access the cue and whether the payoff uses the same fact or affordance. |
| Skipped | Check whether required meaning and the action remain legible. Locate independent evidence where the route must support the inference. |
| Unavailable | Resolve route state, participants and allowed information sources. A participant who never entered a route cannot provide an otherwise missing disclosure. |
| Reordered | Compare disclosure order with event chronology. Check whether a reveal now precedes preparation or a speaker knows a fact too early. |

Classify findings as a missing gate, knowledge leak, weakened motivation or lost payoff. Reachable content can still lack the preparation needed for meaning. Offer an alternative cue, a route-specific payoff or explicit reclassification. State cost, exact source and remaining uncertainty. Ask for player observations when the claimed change is inference or emotional impact.

## Harbor route fixture

The main route reads the relay note and the public warning before operating the latch. The skip route reaches the latch after the public warning only. Both routes support the required action cue. The log adds history about earlier repair pressure.

An adverse draft deletes the public warning and retains unchanged selector availability. The skip route can still reach the selector, but the latch choice loses a legible basis. Reinsert an independent warning or change the selector prompt. Another adverse draft relies on a warden who is absent after a skipped patrol. Resolve participant availability before treating the missing disclosure as an editorial preference.

A reorder can place the latch outcome before the flashback showing earlier preparation. The event date remains stable. Review whether the reordered route retains anticipation or intentionally asks for recognition after the action.
