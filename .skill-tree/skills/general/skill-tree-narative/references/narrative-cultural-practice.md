# Cultural practices through play

Connect a visible practice to a group, material condition, law or event. A practice can preserve shared memory, signal belonging, exchange help or mark an obligation. Pin the place, period and participants before treating a practice as common.

1. Identify an origin and current purpose. Record maintainers, participants and people who contest the practice.
2. Show one observable variation grounded in a changed context. Identify the evidence or mark the variation proposed.
3. Define what participation, refusal or misunderstanding communicates to a specific audience. Let a response provide information, access, support or atmosphere.
4. Keep social response separate from mechanical gating. A decorative cultural detail can stay outside quest state.
5. Review any claim of universal participation against the declared scope and attributed sources.
