# Review character voice under pressure

Use an identity card and three short situations. Compare ordinary conversation, conflict and vulnerability. Keep the same stable voice cues where plausible and explain intentional changes in phrasing, rhythm or directness.

1. Record the immediate goal, counterpart, stakes, route and evidence available before each line.
2. Identify the tactic and relevant value or obligation. Describe vocabulary, sentence length, physical action or deliberate silence.
3. Compare a probable response and a credible alternative. A tendency allows a motivated exception. A physical limitation or hard canon condition requires compatible support.
4. Label findings separately. Unsupported knowledge needs a reachable source or a less specific line. Weak motivation needs a causal bridge. Optional style advice can improve voice without changing events.
5. Rewrite the smallest affected beat and preserve intentional variation between situations.
