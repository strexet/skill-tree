Every mutation requires the inspected queue hash; existing sessions also require session hash and revision. Use clarify-read before resuming; clarify-resume reconciles source drift within the frozen selection, while clarify-cancel records ended discussion without successful required interview evidence. Keep research and waits outside writer locks. Completion reports clarification acceptance and leaves implementation to a separate instruction.
