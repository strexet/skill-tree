# Optional Relay Clue Revision Example

The baseline selector supports all three actions after either log choice. In an adverse proposal, the cooler gate suddenly requires reading the log. The quest still aims to present competing responsibilities under a power limit.

| Stable ID | Baseline | Proposed repair | Recheck |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `scene-relay` | Optional discovery of the trip cause. | Keep the discovery optional. | Discover and skip routes. |
| `power-cooler` | Available after either path. | Supply the diagnostic cue at the selector and keep the action available. | Skip then cool, with visible cue and cold medicine. |
| `power-lift` | Available with a rescue tradeoff. | Keep the cost and feedback. | Rescue as a control. |

Making the log mandatory is a viable alternative if the author chooses mandatory investigation. Adding an alternate cue preserves the declared optionality with less route change. A separate adverse revision adds a fourth outcome but drops a meaningful refusal from the source. Record the lost refusal as a regression and retain author review before adoption.
