# Emergency Selector Outcome Worksheet

| Family | Trigger | Immediate feedback | Durable effect | Next entry |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Success | Power lift. | Workers step down and the nurse removes warm vials. | Crew rescued, medicine warm and pack spent. | Report, then close. |
| Alternative | Power cooler. | Green thermometer and a radio request from the refuge. | Medicine cold, crew waiting and pack spent. | Report, then close. |
| Failure | Try heater. | Fuse blows and the latch closes. | Pack isolated, medicine warm and crew waiting. | Report, then close. |
| Refusal | Decline the selector in a proposed extension. | Ilya records the refusal. | No circuit activated. | Proposed shelter or next playable stage. |
| Skip | Skip optional log inspection. | The selector shows the gauge and split cable. | Trip cause remains unknown and the three actions stay available. | Selector. |
| Re-entry | Check the ledger again. | Existing entry is visible. | The receipt count remains one. | Report or close. |

The refusal row is a design case and has no executable claim. Refusal followed by an inert report would be a dead end. Making lift and cooler grant both safety and cold medicine would erase the stated tradeoff. Resolve the first case with an explicit playable exit and the second with distinct retained state or a revised premise.
