# Lift house location card

The lift house allocates reserve power between the crew lift and clinic cooling. The night emergency starts before the next ordinary service window. The player and Ilya share the selector room. The crew waits in the refuge and the clinic depends on cold stock.

| Place | Access | Verb and feedback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Selector room | Initial access in the emergency | Read the reserve gauge, examine exposed copper and operate a circuit. |
| Relay log station | Optional inspection from the selector room | Read recorded times with Ilya and learn the sequence preceding lightning. |
| Lift platform | Conditional service path after the lift selection | Observe the crew disembark when lift service runs. A cooler selection leaves the crew waiting. |
| Incident desk | Available after a selector outcome | Record the outcome and acknowledge an existing entry on revisit. |

Before selection the green gauge shows usable reserve. After a lift selection the crew arrives and the clinic stock warms. After a heater short the pack isolates without either service. The expected observations follow the pilot route contract.

A player who chooses cooling cannot use the lift-platform rescue route in the same episode. The path contract shows waiting crew and the incident desk as the available outcome, without assuming physical access to the refuge.
