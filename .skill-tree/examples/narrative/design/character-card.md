# Ilya identity card

## Source and scope

`character-ilya` establishes crew priority and an initial storm explanation. `scene-relay` changes Ilya's belief only on inspection. The role, relationships and tactics below are synthetic development proposals for the lift-house episode.

| Field | Proposed profile |
| --- | --- |
| Position and limits | Lift-house operator with access to the selector area. The reserve allows one high-draw service. |
| Immediate goal | Bring the crew out of the refuge before the wait becomes more dangerous. |
| Durable value | Protect people sharing the work. Keep service decisions accountable. |
| History and relationships | The failed heater lead and interruption make equipment reliability immediate. A proposed duty to report losses gives the clinic liaison leverage over the wording of the incident entry. |
| Knowledge and belief | Ilya believes the storm damaged the pack before reading the relay log. Inspection supplies recorded ordering. Skip-route discussion can cite visible copper and the gauge without citing unseen times. |
| Tactics and voice | A protective response asks for crew location before debating blame. An accountable response names the loss and records the selected service in terse equipment terms. |
| Arc | Ilya can remain committed to the crew while changing the explanation of the fault. A later work can change the method or role without requiring a personal trauma or a victory. |

## Worked choice

Ilya argues for lift service because the crew is waiting and the operator values immediate rescue. A cooling choice can still receive a disciplined log entry because Ilya values a record of harm and cannot restore the spent reserve. A request for unquestioning obedience weakens the causal profile. Replace the label "loyal" with the observable request for a refuge status check and the reason for making the request.
