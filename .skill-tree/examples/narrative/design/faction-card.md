# Ridge maintenance circle

The Ridge maintenance circle is a design fixture proposal. The circle maintains the lift-house equipment and can share relay records or request repairs. The circle cannot create another reserve cycle. The public aim is safe continuity of service. A steward may claim universal agreement on crew priority. The statement remains attributed to the steward.

| Position | Incentive and membership | Cooling-choice response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platform steward | Rescue obligations and the reputation of the lift crew keep the steward in the circle. | Logs concern about delayed rescue and asks for a visible refuge check. |
| Clinic liaison | Repair training and clinic supply duty keep the liaison in the circle. | Acknowledges protected stock and offers the maintenance ledger for follow-up. |

The player can request ledger access after recording the chosen circuit. The offer depends on the liaison and access authority. The steward cannot reverse the recorded outcome. A claim that every member opposed cooling conflicts with the two position descriptions and must remain a claim from a named speaker or an open question.
