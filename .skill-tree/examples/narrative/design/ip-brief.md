# Ridge Pack IP brief

## Starting premise

A mountain lift house has a reserve pack with enough power for one high-draw cycle. The player can use the reserve to rescue a stranded crew or protect clinic stock. The desired experience is an informed tradeoff under time pressure.

The starting material is the synthetic pilot brief and the `law-pack`, `quest-ridge` and `scene-relay` records. The brief uses the existing mountain setting and selector. The brief treats fault attribution as an open proposal.

## Creative pillars

| Pillar | Observable expression |
| --- | --- |
| Scarcity forces a decision | The gauge and selector make one remaining high-draw cycle visible before selection. |
| Investigation improves understanding | Relay inspection explains the unsafe heater lead. The gauge and visible damage still give usable feedback on the skip route. |
| Consequences are local and legible | A rescue disembarks the crew and warms the stock. Cooling keeps the stock cold and leaves the crew waiting. |

## Small episode

The episode starts in the lift house with the player and Ilya. The player may inspect the relay log and then chooses a circuit. A short feedback beat shows the chosen service and the cost to the other service. The episode stops at the incident logbook. The scope uses one location, one decision and the existing pilot routes.

## Decision frontier

The current fixture keeps a mechanical fault. An agent can propose a follow-up about responsibility without extending the first episode. A request for both a fixed crew rescue and a genuinely player-determined crew fate needs an author decision. Offer a fixed rescue with an independent cost choice or a variable crew outcome. Hold outcome-dependent work while drafting signs, inspection and neutral selector feedback.
