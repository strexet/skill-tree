# The Ridge pause

The Ridge pause is a proposed practice inspired by the single-service emergency. The lift crew records which service received power and pauses before handing over the logbook. The act recognizes the cost imposed on the other group. A platform steward maintains the practice. Some clinic staff prefer to record stock loss immediately.

At the lift house, the pause occurs with hands off the selector. In a proposed later clinic shift, a stock handler taps the empty container rack before recording loss. The difference follows the station duty and clinic inventory context. Neither practice is an accepted fact outside the fixture.

| Interaction | Proposed visible response |
| --- | --- |
| Join the station pause | The steward names the crew still waiting or the stock sacrificed, making the unchosen cost explicit. |
| Decline the pause and record immediately | The steward gives a terse acknowledgment and directs the player to the incident entry. Access to the logbook remains available. |
| Mistake the clinic tap for a selector command | The stock handler identifies the rack as a memory cue. No power state changes. |

The refuge thunder retelling and the pause can coexist as different uses of the trip event. A scarf color can remain an atmospheric variation. An assertion that every clinic member performs the station gesture would need evidence or an author decision.
