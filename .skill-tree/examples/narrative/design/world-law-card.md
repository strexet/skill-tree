# Reserve pack law card

## Physical rule

`law-pack` supplies one high-draw cycle or enough power for one low-draw heater. A short isolates the pack before service. Reset requires a spare fuse. The rule applies to the lift-house reserve during the Ridge Pack episode. The capacity check, reserve gauge, relay log and exposed copper supply different evidence. Only the inspection route shows the log chronology.

| Clause | Condition | Player-visible consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capacity | Charge remains and the chosen circuit is sound | Selecting lift or cooling consumes the high-draw cycle. The chosen service runs. |
| Isolation | A short remains in the heater circuit | A blown fuse and isolation stop delivery before the heater runs. |
| Reset | Isolation has occurred and a spare fuse is available | The reset can restore selector access. The current event record establishes use of the last spare before the episode. |

## Norm and preference

For a training variant, a posted station rule reserves a sound cooler for an approved clinic allocation. A player without allocation may physically activate a sound cooler and trigger an accountability response. The response remains a proposal. An exhausted pack cannot run the same cooler regardless of allocation. Ilya prefers crew rescue. The preference can influence an argument without changing capacity.
