# Ridge object card

## Reserve pack

The reserve pack is maintained station equipment and appears as a green gauge beside the selector. The gauge shows available charge. The pack represents emergency responsibility to the lift crew and clinic. The gauge alone establishes neither circuit safety nor fault chronology.

The pack is accessible from the selector room at the start of the episode. `law-pack` establishes one high-draw cycle or sufficient power for a low-draw heater. A short isolates the pack before service. A reset requires a spare fuse.

| Setup | Use | Payoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The green gauge shows charge and the selector exposes the alternatives. | Select a sound circuit while the pack has charge. | The selected service runs and the unused service loses the available allocation. |
| Optional relay inspection or exposed copper identifies a heater risk. | Attempt the unsafe heater. | The short isolates the pack before the heater runs. |

The blue seal supports observation and interpretation. The design assigns no state effect to a decorative wall pennant.

## Adverse proposal

A late reset scene promises a second service. `event-trip` records use of the last spare fuse before the episode. Without a new source of possession or an approved rule change, the reset payoff is unsupported. The proposal can introduce a prior opportunity to obtain a spare or retain the current isolation outcome. A decorative object cannot serve as a spare by implication.
