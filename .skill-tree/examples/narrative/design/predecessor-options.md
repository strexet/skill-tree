## Incompatible request

A later opening that assumes immediate crew rescue and preserved clinic stock combines mutually exclusive outcomes from one reserve allocation. Keep the policy unresolved. Offer the fixed lift route, the fixed cooling route or a common-facts opening without either outcome. A revised work with additional rescue and cooling resources requires a separately reviewed world change.
